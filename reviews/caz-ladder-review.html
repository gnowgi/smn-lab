01 · Where we are

Three rungs built — and the central primitive finally pinned down

This thread built and corrected the first three rungs of the ladder (cnidarian → nematode → earthworm), grounded each in sourced biology, and consolidated the two new morphologies plus their docs onto main. It then did the more important thing: it settled what a CAZ is, and the settlement dissolves the confusion this report opened on.

The resolution

The earlier finding — “CAZ names three incompatible things in the code” — was real, but it was a symptom. The cause was calling the contractile unit a CAZ. Separate them, and it resolves: the edge is an f (a pull-only contractile unit); the CAZ is not an actuator at all but a zone of coordinated action — and coordination is a property of the whole SMN, not of the neurons.

02 · The settled understanding

Coordination is the SMN’s, not the neurons’

The load-bearing commitment, above the definition: coordination is a property of the SMN as a whole — the coupled network of sensing, pulling, and messaging units — not of any one kind of unit. The received view hands coordination to the neurons. SMN denies it: messaging carries signals but coordinates nothing on its own, pulling units can’t time themselves, sensing alone is mute. Coordination is what the three do together, closed through the body’s mechanics and its own reafference. There is no coordinator inside the system.

So the ontology has four levels, and the CAZ is derived, not a component:

  • s — segment: a node (a body region).
  • f — contractile unit: an edge; it can only pull. A spindle is a bundle of fs taken as one — so f is scale-recursive.
  • opposition: the resistance every f pulls against (elastic, hydrostat, the network, or a discrete antagonist). Mechanical, always present — its form differentiates up the ladder.
  • CAZ: the coordination of the timing of the fs — which act together, which after which — realized by the whole SMN and read out, never imposed.
The definition, settled

A CAZ is a zone of coordinated action — a set of contractile units brought into a coherent timing relation (synchronous, sequential, or opposed) by the SMN as a whole: sensing + pulling + messaging, closed through the body and its reafference. It is emergent and read out, never imposed by a controller; the discrete opponent pair is its fully-differentiated limit.

Two consequences fall straight out. Sensing is now on the critical path — the coordination loop cannot close without transducers, so they are no longer an add-on for the Umwelt finale. And coordination must emerge from that loop: our current earthworm/nematode demos script the wave phases, which is the neuron-as-controller shortcut in disguise — the faithful version couples each pull to what it senses and lets the gait fall out.

04 · v2 → v3 · the paper

Where the manuscript still commits “CAZ = a pair”

The authoritative arXiv v2 defines the CAZ as an opponent pair — yet also calls it “a network from the word go.” v3 resolves that internal tension toward the network reading; these are the passages to generalize (not discard: opponency stays the primitive, and the pair stays the haltable unit).

v2 · §3.4, p7 — the definition “A Coordinated Action Zone (CAZ) is a pair of Sensation Modulators in antagonistic relationship — two zones Z⁺ and Z⁻ … together with … the communication board B.”
v3: CAZ = a zone of coordinated action over a set of Sensation Modulators, coordinated by the board(s) and the whole SMN; the antagonistic pair (Z⁺, Z⁻, B) is its minimal, haltable case. Keep the board inside the CAZ; keep “smallest coherent/haltable scale” as the definition of the pair.
v2 · §11, p38 — the contradiction to exploit “the CAZ is a network from the word go” · “the CAZ is the structural-and-functional unit of cognition.”
v3: §11 already says network; §3.4 says pair. Reconcile in favour of §11 — an internal-consistency fix, the cleanest mandate for the change.
v2 · §4.2, p11 — skeleton typing “a hydrostatic CAZ (worm, tongue, gut) is a pair of linked linear actuators … whose antagonist is the structure itself.”
v3: v2 already types worms/hydrostats — but still as a pair. Add a third type: distributed / network opposition (the cnidarian — a region contracts against the whole connected sheet), a coordinated CAZ with no discrete pair. This is the case that forces the generalization.

Figure 2 (“CAZ = one opponent pair = one DOF”), §4.4 (“an opponent pair of actuators, one CAZ”), and the commitment to add — “coordination is the SMN’s, not the neurons’” — are itemized in SMN_v3_caz_revision_flags.md, with the paper of record at papers/preprint/.

06 · What the settlement changes — next steps

Now the work is concrete

With the definition settled, the choices are no longer conceptual — they’re a build order.

Build the shared substrate: pull-only f + sensing + emergent coordination

Recommended

One pull-only f primitive for every body; sensing units as first-class; and coordination that emerges from the sense→message→pull→mechanics loop rather than a scripted schedule. This is the honest version of the commitment and the “same model in all morphologies,” and it retires the three-way muddle in one move. The crawler’s opponent pair is the seed to generalize off the hinge.

Leverage highest · Effort medium–large · replaces the scripted waves

Revise the paper for v3

Reconcile §3.4 with §11 to the general definition, add the “coordination is the SMN’s, not the neurons’” commitment and a distributed-opposition CAZ type, and demote the opponent pair to the differentiated limit that carries haltability — keeping every opponency and haltability result intact. Flags ready in the project doc.
